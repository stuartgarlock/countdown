# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Countdown is published by SMG Group LLC.

**Countdown collects no data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking,
and it makes no network connections at all.

- **Your events** (titles, dates, notes, colours) are stored only on your device, in a file the
  app shares with its own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. They are included in your device's
  backups (iCloud or computer) in the same way as any other app's data, under your Apple account,
  and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **Deleting the app** deletes your events.

Because we never receive any of your information, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or
delete on request.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/stuartgarlock/countdown/issues.
